Privacy policy

This policy covers the Elora Wellness app for iPhone and iPad. You don’t need an account to listen or practise breathing.

What stays on your device

Elora stores your favourites, preferences, listening time and breathing session count on your device. The app does not send this information to the developer or use it for advertising or tracking. To clear this local information, delete the app rather than offloading it.

If you email support

Contacting support is optional. If you write to us, your email address and anything you choose to include reach our support mailbox, hosted by Gmail. We use this information to respond to your enquiry. We keep support emails only while we are working on your enquiry. Once it is resolved, we delete the correspondence unless the law requires us to keep it. To ask for a support conversation to be deleted, email eloraquietspace@gmail.com.
